On The Fence? Try The Demo!

SteamVR and Oculus users can visit our itch.io page to try a free demo of Headmaster! The demo is a great way to get a quick taste for the gameplay, humor, and atmosphere of the full game. This demo features several single-player levels from the campaign, as well as one local-multiplayer party mode level to try with friends.

PSVR users can also access a Headmaster demo by downloading either the PlayStation VR Demo Disc or the PlayStation VR Demo Disc 3.
