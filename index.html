A Unique Game only possible in VR

As its name suggests, in Headmaster you head virtual soccer balls (and other things) with your actual head. Combining intuitive physics gameplay with a darkly comedic world, this bizarre love child of Portal and Wii Sports has been giving Playstation VR owners fits of joy since it launched. Recently Headmaster came to PC via Steam and Oculus.

Features:

  • Single player story campaign with over 40 levels
  • Local Party Mode with 11 levels and up to 6 player pass and play
  • Unique VR-only mechanic where you use your head for gameplay.
  • Prior experience or interest in soccer is NOT required!
